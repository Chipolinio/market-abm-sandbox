# Research Questions — Spec 015 / paper draft

## RQ1

How does increasing the share of ML sellers (CatBoost repricing) affect **price volatility** and **market concentration (HHI)** before, during, and after a macro demand shock?

## RQ2

Does algorithmic pricing redistribute **welfare** among consumer surplus (budget-residual proxy), producer surplus, and platform profit as `ml_seller_share` rises?

## Working hypotheses

- **H1:** At high ML share, price volatility rises in stress relative to rules-only (0% ML).
- **H2:** Producer surplus gains come at the expense of consumer surplus proxy and/or platform profit — direction is empirical from the ablation grid.
