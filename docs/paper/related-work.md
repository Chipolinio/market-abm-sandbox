# Related Work (stub)

Short map of adjacent literatures. Expand to ~1 page in the paper.

## Algorithmic pricing and collusion

- **Calvano, Calzolari, Denicolò, Pastorello (2020).** Algorithmic pricing and tacit collusion in Q-learning experiments — key benchmark for “do pricing algorithms soften competition?”

## Discrete choice / demand

- **Berry, Levinsohn, Pakes (1995) / BLP.** Random-coefficients discrete choice for differentiated products — methodological ancestor of MNL-style buyer choice used here (simplified, without full BLP instruments).

## Agent-based computational economics

- **Tesfatsion (2006)** and ACE surveys — ABM as a laboratory for market institutions; motivates ODD documentation and careful calibration tables.

## Marketplace ranking and reputation

- Platform ranking / reputation dynamics (e-commerce empirics) motivate Spec 012 ranking weights and dynamic ratings — cite field studies in the full draft.
